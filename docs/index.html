From airfoil to workshop

One model. Every part.

DesignRC keeps the aerodynamic definition, structure, 3D preview, plans, and exported parts connected—so a design change flows through the entire wing.

01

Parametric wing geometry

Define connected panels with independent chord, sweep, twist, dihedral, rib spacing, and root and tip airfoils. Import dimensioned SVG/DXF leading and trailing edges for curved panels. DesignRC mirrors the half-wing into the complete aircraft.

02

Built-up structure

Generate ribs, rib caps, root-to-tip spars, shear webs, front, rear, and trailing-edge sheeting, collision-checked joiners, spoilers, and solid or built-up controls with matching hinge posts or beveled top/bottom hinge spars.

03

True solid preview

Inspect the assembled wing in an interactive OpenCascade viewport with orbit, pan, zoom, material colors, and STEP export.

04

Manufacturing output

Export the complete full-scale plan as PDF, RGB-colored DXF, or SVG with colors and transparency. Export selected laser-cut parts as vector DXF, SVG, or PDF files.

05

Exact workshop units

Work globally in millimeters or inches, override units per parameter, and display exact 1/32-inch values as familiar fractions.

New in version 1.7.0 · November 3, 2026

Build the controls. Export the plan.

Design separate aileron and flap ribs, choose the hinge construction, set joiner span by rib bays, and export a full-scale plan in PDF, DXF, or SVG.

01

Built-up ailerons and flaps

Add separate ribs, independent top/bottom skins, and wood or carbon spars. Choose matching Hinge Posts or four Top/Bottom Hinge Spars with an aft bevel. Hinge-spar skins automatically reach their spars, and the wing's TE settings remain independent. Control ribs use ARn/FRn and stock TE parts use ATE/FTE names throughout plans and manufacturing output. New parameters are saved in projects and available in Edit > Defaults.

02

Colored full-scale plan exports

Export Plan offers PDF, DXF, or SVG before choosing a filename. DXF retains RGB line and label colors; SVG also retains fill colors and transparency. Outlined labels preserve their shape without installed fonts. Plans include leaders for control spars, sheeting thickness, and TE stock dimensions. CAD sketch display colors may override colors stored in an imported file.

03

Joiner bays and clearer geometry checks

Number of Rib Bays controls fixed wood, tube, and rod joiners and removable sleeves/rods. One bay passes through two ribs, and the selected span must fit both panels. Alignment pins keep their existing lengths. Checks identify joiners leaving rib boundaries; stock height equality, deleted spars, shared control gaps, and exported sheeting notches are handled consistently.

A direct path to fabrication

Design. Inspect. Export.

Move from imported airfoil coordinates to build-ready parts without rebuilding the same geometry in separate CAD and drawing tools.

    Set panel geometry, import Selig-style airfoils, and configure the internal structure.

  3. 03

    Make the parts

    Export full-scale plans, cutting files, and the complete material-colored assembly.

  4. 04

    Continue in CAD

    Download the STEP file and import the wing assembly into your favorite CAD tool.
