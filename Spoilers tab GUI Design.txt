Geometric Processing

A spoiler consists of a rectangular shape with width measured chord wise and the Thickness measured from the top of the ribs down.
A Notch is cut in "Start Rib" + 1 through "End Rib" - 1 and starting at the "Start Chord Location" and extending (2 * "Frame Rail Width" + 1/8in + "Spoiler Width") towards the TE.  It is "Spoiler Thickness Deep".  The 3D parts are in the notches cut starting at the "Start Chord Location" towards the TE are shown below:
  
    Frame Rail 1
    1/16in gap
    Spoiler
    1/16in gap
    Frame Rail 2

The Spoiler extends from the inside edge of the "Start Rib" to the "End Rib" with a 1/16in gap between the spoiler and the ribs.
The Frame Rails extend the same with no gaps.  They run right up to the ribs.
Under the ends of the spoiler/Frame Rails, up against the ribs on both ends, is a "Support Rail" that runs chordwise the extent of the Frame Rails and Spoiler.  It is glued under the spoiler/frame rails and to the inside surface of the end ribs and supports the ends of the spoiler/Frame Rails.
Create the geometry and show the part names and Dimensions on the plan.  The Spoiler is the only part that gets exported.

Special Case:  If the Start Rib is 1, then the Spoiler and Frame Rails must be extruded as one piece from the inside of the End Rib to End Rib on both wing halves.  There should only be one spoiler part to export.  Annotations need only appear on one wing half in the plan.
